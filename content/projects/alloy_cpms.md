Aim of the project was to derive functional and non-functional requirements for an electric vehicle's *Charge-Point Management System* (CPMS), as well as designing a valid architecture. Such properties were then formally verified with Alloy.
Finally, IEEE-compliant [Requirements Analysis, Specification] and [Design] Documents have been produced.
